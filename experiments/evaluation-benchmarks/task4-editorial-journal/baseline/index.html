The Architecture of Silence: Spatial Contemplation in Modernist Prose

By Dr. Elena Vance · 14 min read · Literary Theory

Modernism did not merely dismantle rhetorical ornamentation; it reorganized the cadence of cognitive pause. When prose ceases to rush toward closure, space itself becomes a syntactical element.

"The punctuation of space carries equal weight to the sentence itself; what remains unsaid structures the room." — Spatial Poetics, Vance (2024)

To examine this requires reconsidering the linear progression of narrative momentum.
